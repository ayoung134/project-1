Award Winning Key Lime Pie!

I make this pie every year for Thanksgiving and Christmas, and believe me when I tell you it is so good! It's sweet, tangy and just the right amount of tartness, the kind of pie that disappears faster then you can say "save me a slice."

Ingredients

Crust

  • 2 cups of graham cracker crumbs
  • 5 tbsp sugar
  • 6 tbsp

Filling

  • 2 - 14 oz cans of sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 3/4 cup key lime juice
  • 2 egg yolks
  • Zest from 1 lime

Whipped Cream

  • 8 oz heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp
  • 3-4 tbsp powdered sugar

Instructions

Crust

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees.
  2. Pulse graham crackers in your food processor until they become crumbs.
  3. Add in sugar, butter, and pulse until combined.
  4. Press crumbs onto your pie plate, across the bottom and up the sides.
  5. Bake for 6-8 minutes or until golden brown.
  6. Allow crust to cool completely.

Filling

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees.
  2. Combine all imgredients in your mixing bowl and allow them to beat on med-high for 2 minutes.
  3. Scrape filling into your prepared pie crust.
  4. Bake for 15-20 minutes or until filling only wiggles a bit in the center when you gently shake the pan back and forth a bit.
  5. Don't allow pie to brown.
  6. Cool for at least 30 minutes, refridgerate for at least 3 hours.

Whipped Cream

  1. In your mixing bowl, whip the heavy whipping cream on high until stiff peaks begin to form.
  2. Add in vanilla and powdered sugar and whip until lfirm whipped cream happens.
  3. Spread onto cooled pie using a spatula and decorate your pie.
  4. Keep pie chilled until serving.

Notes

I normally juice my lime and incldue the 3/4 cup of key lime juice, just to have that extra bit of tartness. If you want to just buy a pre-made graham cracker crust I would reccomend getting the 10" becuase the 9" is to small and you will have some left over. Unless you want some left over to "taste test", just to make sure its edible.
